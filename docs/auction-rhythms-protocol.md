# Auction House Rhythms

The public article is [Rythmes de l’hôtel des ventes](https://mana-mechanica.com/?lang=fr&page=forever-auction-house-rhythms).
It uses the current Price Observatory watchlist, with the same item IDs, local icons and localized names. Use the full AHledger market ID to keep regions, rulesets and factions distinct. Alliance Normal EU is the default. The page is available in French, English and Spanish.

## Sources and coverage

- The Sites read-only endpoint is `GET /api/auction-rhythms?market=<market>&item=<itemId>`. Without parameters it returns the supported Forever market combinations advertised by AHledger. A listed combination can have zero observations.
- [AHledger API](https://ahledger.com/developers), `prices/{market}/{item}?range=24h`, supplies exact source-timed scans. The `7d` range supplies hourly UTC buckets and can also identify a latest exact scan. Validate response market, item and range before admitting a point. The page links the “Prices from AHledger” credit to the item’s market page where provided.
- The existing Tensions archive and Market Bulletin contribute available historical points for the selected market and item. Legacy observations from the Tensions collector at a full UTC hour, without an explicit kind, are conservatively treated as hourly aggregates. The two historical views count exact source times and hourly bucket starts separately. Daily UTC aggregates are excluded.
- The existing EU Normal Alliance archive contributes 68,923 distinct source-dated records for 75 items, 5–17 November 2026 UTC. It combines the original Observatory import with the saved exact 24-hour series. The only imported columns are item ID, UTC observation time, unit minimum and median, and listed quantity. Historical heatmap cells are excluded from the input. There is no new scheduled archive collector.
- Deduplicate by market, item, granularity and UTC timestamp. Prefer a verified current source value and retain an available older field if the newer response leaves it empty. Keep quantity zero separate from missing and display a source failure with any available records. Date each point by the source, independently of the page load or calculation.
- Respect AHledger cache headers. AHledger announces a revised retention rule from 10 December 2026. Keeping API responses beyond the allowed cache duration or maintaining an archive after that change requires checking the archive licence. Historical exact coverage for other markets or later watchlist additions depends on the existing history and current provider data.

## Calculation and display

- Prices are unit buyouts in copper. A measured mean from a complete listing cohort uses sum(unit price × listed units) / sum(listed units); grouped lots multiply lot size by lot count first. The checked feeds carry neither a verified weighted mean nor a complete lot ladder. Show “Not measured” for mean and the volume at the minimum. If one of these fields becomes verifiable, capture its source timestamp and record its coverage start before displaying it.
- Keep UTC internally. Use the AHledger market reference zones Europe/Paris, America/Los_Angeles, Asia/Shanghai and Asia/Seoul across charts, day boundaries, dates and scenario time slots. Display the applicable time-zone offset or abbreviation.
- The recent periods are rolling 21-day, 3-day and 3-hour windows ending at viewing time. The full view starts at its layer’s first available point. Report the last exact scan independently of the selected window. All charts, tables, heatmaps and day pairs use the same selected layer and period. Plot observed points, including isolated scans, and break lines across gaps.
- Daily rows use the lowest observed minimum, arithmetic mean of available weighted means, median of observed medians, and median/min/max stock. Show point counts and incomplete time-slot coverage. Day and time-slot aggregates describe listed stock.
- Derive slot width from the median observed interval, in 1-, 3-, 6-, 12- or 24-hour bins. Each weekday/slot cell uses the median of daily slot medians and counts distinct local dates.
- Chart density adapts to the plot width. Dense price and stock traces omit routine point markers and compress each time bucket to its first, last, low and high measured points in timestamp order. Missing values and time gaps still split the line. Isolated points stay visible. This is a display operation over the existing observations.
- Price scale offers Auto, Full range and Typical prices. With dense data and at least 20 price values, Auto uses the typical scale if the full axis spans at least three times the typical axis. The typical axis uses the 5th and 95th percentiles of the displayed series with a small margin. Triangles mark values outside the axis. Full range displays the full extent. The cursor and tables use the original prices and dates in every mode.
- The chart cursor snaps to an available observation and shares its exact UTC instant across price and stock plots. Each chart shows the same date, time and abbreviation in the market reference zone. Pointer, touch and the keyboard slider use the active period and granularity. A missing measure stays missing at the selected instant.
- Use the 17–18 h local window as a hypothesis from [RageGamingVideos at 6:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JHYlSuO_dJI&t=379s). Within each selected window take the observed price closest to that date’s slot median, using minimum or median for buying and median for resale. Group by local weekday. The arithmetic means use one observation per date for each window and display each window’s count. A date can contribute to one window, while margins require a later resale observation on the same date.
- The simulator takes a quantity, units per listing and a count of failed attempts over the full quantity. Enter the deposit shown in game for a full listing of that size and duration, and the separate deposit for the remainder when needed. A batch deposits (number of full listings × full listing deposit) + remainder deposit. Multiply by the count of full attempts without a sale to compute lost deposits. Zero failed attempts gives zero loss. A required missing amount leaves the loss and margin unmeasured. The final successful sale returns its deposit.
- Blizzard announced a floor change with the [November 17 maintenance](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-16-2026/2403040/7). [Recorded WoW Forever auctions](https://wowfah.com/guides/wow-forever-auction-house-cut-and-deposit) show that lot sizes and refunds affect actual deposits. Use the live in-game amount for the item, lot and duration. Keep the copper value editable by the reader.
- The total simulated margin equals quantity × (assumed resale price after commission − purchase price) − lost deposits. Commission defaults to 5%, with an editable percentage and fractional copper retained for the scenario. The median and positive share use dates with calculable margins. Keep known stock shortages visible in the paired dates and exclude them from the margin measures. When volume at the quote is missing, the scenario assumes availability at that price and labels that assumption for multiple units.
- Compare the latest visible point with medians from past dates in the same local slot and weekday/weekend group. Require four qualifying dates per comparison. Price-rise/stock-drop thresholds are +20% and +1 copper, and −25% and −10 units. High stock requires at least twice the typical stock and 20 additional units. The low-minimum scarcity signal uses −30% and verified volume at the minimum of at most 5 units or 5% of stock.

## Historical comparison across items

The published comparison is a dated study of Europe Normal Alliance. At build time, the generator combines the Observatory’s bundled source-timed scans with source-timed AHledger scans from the bulletin inside that archive window. It deduplicates each item's timestamps and filters to the current watchlist. Report the local date interval, market, available item count and usable matched counts separately from the interactive, live item selector.

Rank in copper per item for one simulated unit, with a 5% faction commission and zero lost deposit. Entry is the listed minimum. Resale uses a later listed median on the same local date. Ignore explicit zero stocks. The single-date table searches all ordered same-day pairs and retains the highest simulated margin per item. The repeated table uses the price closest to each three-hour slot's median, reuses the simulator's formula, and takes the median net margin over at least four matched dates. Include the actual dates, observation times, positive dates and median percentage return. For each item keep its best eligible scenario and then rank those results.

Assess the timing tendency independently with median-price entry from 06:00 to 09:00 and median-price resale windows 12:00–15:00, 15:00–18:00, 17:00–18:00 and 18:00–21:00. Compare the same dates within each item. Require three dates with all windows and weight items equally by their median percentage return. Report the number of items with positive median returns as well as the overall median. The rankings and the timing study describe listed prices in the dated sample.
