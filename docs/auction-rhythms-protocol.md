# Auction House Rhythms

The public article is [Rythmes de l’hôtel des ventes](https://mana-mechanica.com/?lang=fr&page=forever-auction-house-rhythms).
It uses the current Price Observatory watchlist, with the same item IDs, local icons and localized names. Use the full AHledger market ID to keep regions, rulesets and factions distinct. Alliance Normal EU is the default. The page is available in French, English and Spanish.

## Sources and coverage

- The Sites read-only endpoint is `GET /api/auction-rhythms?market=<market>&item=<itemId>`. Without parameters it returns the supported Forever market combinations advertised by AHledger. A listed combination can have zero observations.
- The comparison requests `GET /api/auction-rhythms?market=<market>&period=<period>` for one market’s watchlist archive and bulletin series. The period is `all`, `30d`, `21d`, `7d`, `3d`, `48h`, `24h` or `3h`. It reads the selected archive window in pages of 4,000 records, without the former 100,000-record cutoff; per-item reads also continue beyond the former 8,000-record cutoff. A failed page marks the response partial and keeps the measured records already read. No per-item provider requests are made by the comparison.
- [AHledger API](https://ahledger.com/developers), `prices/{market}/{item}?range=24h`, supplies exact source-timed scans. The `7d` range supplies hourly UTC buckets and can also identify a latest exact scan. Validate response market, item and range before admitting a point. The page links the “Prices from AHledger” credit to the item’s market page where provided.
- The existing Tensions archive and Market Bulletin contribute available historical points for the selected market and item. Legacy observations from the Tensions collector at a full UTC hour, without an explicit kind, are conservatively treated as hourly aggregates. The two historical views count exact source times and hourly bucket starts separately. Daily UTC aggregates are excluded.
- The existing EU Normal Alliance archive contributes 70,890 distinct source-dated records for402 items, 5–20 November2026 UTC. It combines the original Observatory import with the saved exact 24-hour series. The only imported columns are item ID, UTC observation time, unit minimum and median, and listed quantity. Historical heatmap cells are excluded from the input. This dated import remains intact. The hourly collector now enriches it through rotating seven-day history recovery, described in the hourly protocol.
- Deduplicate by market, item, granularity and UTC timestamp. Prefer a verified current source value and retain an available older field if the newer response leaves it empty. Keep quantity zero separate from missing and display a source failure with any available records. Date each point by the source, independently of the page load or calculation.
- Respect AHledger cache headers. AHledger announces a revised retention rule from 10 December 2026. Keeping API responses beyond the allowed cache duration or maintaining an archive after that change requires checking the archive licence. Historical exact coverage for other markets or later watchlist additions depends on the existing history and current provider data.

## Calculation and display

- Prices are unit buyouts in copper. A measured mean from a complete listing cohort uses sum(unit price × listed units) / sum(listed units); grouped lots multiply lot size by lot count first. The checked feeds carry neither a verified weighted mean nor a complete lot ladder. When a chart lacks a mean in the selected period, show a text status with a short explanation about quantities at each price. Use the checkbox only for a measured series. Display “Not measured” for missing means and volume at the minimum. If a new field becomes verifiable, capture its source timestamp and record its coverage start before displaying it.
- Keep UTC internally. Use the AHledger market reference zones Europe/Paris, America/Los_Angeles, Asia/Shanghai and Asia/Seoul across charts, day boundaries, dates and scenario time slots. Display the applicable time-zone offset or abbreviation.
- The recent chart periods are rolling 30-day, 21-day, 7-day, 3-day, 48-hour, 24-hour and 3-hour windows ending at viewing time. The full view starts at its layer’s first available point. Report the last exact scan independently of the selected window. The charts, local-day table and individual observations use the chart period. The heatmap, weekday averages and simulator share a period control and default to the available history for the selected market, watchlist item and observation type. Readers can select the chart period for these views too. Display the data span and number of local dates for this weekly sample. Connect consecutive available observations for each metric with straight segments, including across longer intervals.
- Collapse individual observations by default. Introduce the list as timestamped prices, listed units and sources. Paginate the detail from the most recent observation and report the number of observations.
- Daily rows use the lowest observed minimum, arithmetic mean of available weighted means, median of observed medians, and median/min/max stock. Show point counts and incomplete time-slot coverage. Day and time-slot aggregates describe listed stock.
- Derive slot width from the median observed interval, in 1-, 3-, 6-, 12- or 24-hour bins. Show all seven days and every time slot in the heatmap. Each cell uses the median of daily slot medians and counts distinct local dates. A slot without a measured value shows a dash and zero dates. Preserve a measured stock of zero as a value.
- Chart density adapts to the plot width. Dense price and stock traces omit routine point markers and include every measured point in timestamp order. A series with a single measured point displays a marker. This is a display operation over the existing observations.
- Price scale offers Auto, Full range and Typical prices. With dense data and at least 20 price values, Auto uses the typical scale if the full axis spans at least three times the typical axis. The typical axis uses the 5th and 95th percentiles of the displayed series with a small margin. Triangles mark values outside the axis. Full range displays the full extent. The cursor and tables use the original prices and dates in every mode.
- The chart cursor snaps to an available observation and shares its exact UTC instant across price and stock plots. Each chart shows the same date, time and abbreviation in the market reference zone. Pointer, touch and the keyboard slider use the active period and granularity. A missing measure stays missing at the selected instant.
- The enlarged chart view uses the same curves and synchronized cursor. Zoom centers on the selected observation and halves or doubles the number of visible points. Earlier and later move by half a window. Axes retain the timestamps of the observations in view. Closing the dialog restores the full chart period with focus on the button.
- Use the 17–18 h local window as a hypothesis from [RageGamingVideos at 6:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JHYlSuO_dJI&t=379s). Within each window take the observed price closest to that date’s slot median, using minimum or median for buying and median for resale. The weekday averages always show all seven rows, including gaps. Their arithmetic means count one price per local date for each window and display both counts. A date can contribute to one average even if the other window has no observation.
- Choose purchase and resale weekdays separately. Two different weekdays pair each purchase date with the first scheduled local resale date after it, including across the end of the week. If either weekday is “All days”, or both weekdays are the same, pair within each eligible purchase date using a later, nonoverlapping resale window. Require an observation in both windows and a resale timestamp after the purchase. Missing scheduled resale dates stay unmatched. Count scenario pairs by purchase weekday and display both observation dates and times in the expandable results.
- The simulator takes a quantity, units per listing and a count of failed attempts over the full quantity. Enter the deposit shown in game for a full listing of that size and duration, and the separate deposit for the remainder when needed. A batch deposits (number of full listings × full listing deposit) + remainder deposit. Multiply by the count of full attempts without a sale to compute lost deposits. Zero failed attempts gives zero loss. A required missing amount leaves the loss and margin unmeasured. The final successful sale returns its deposit.
- Blizzard announced a floor change with the [November 17 maintenance](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-16-2026/2403040/7). [Recorded WoW Forever auctions](https://wowfah.com/guides/wow-forever-auction-house-cut-and-deposit) show that lot sizes and refunds affect actual deposits. Use the live in-game amount for the item, lot and duration. Keep the copper value editable by the reader.
- The total simulated margin equals quantity × (assumed resale price after commission − purchase price) − lost deposits. Commission defaults to 5%, with an editable percentage and fractional copper retained for the scenario. The median and positive share use dates with calculable margins. Keep known stock shortages visible in the paired dates and exclude them from the margin measures. When volume at the quote is missing, the scenario assumes availability at that price and labels that assumption for multiple units.
- Compare the latest visible point with medians from past dates in the same local slot and weekday/weekend group. Require four qualifying dates per comparison. Price-rise/stock-drop thresholds are +20% and +1 copper, and −25% and −10 units. High stock requires at least twice the typical stock and 20 additional units. The low-minimum scarcity signal uses −30% and verified volume at the minimum of at most 5 units or 5% of stock.

Report the number of items with positive median returns as well as the overall median. The rankings and the timing study describe listed prices in the dated sample.

## Provider corrections and recovery · 21 November 2026

The server stores source-timed hourly aggregates separately from exact latest scans. SHA-256 value versions retain provider corrections; canonical reading selects the most recently received version for each market, item, type and instant. A provider reverting to an older archived value updates that version’s last-received metadata while preserving its first retrieval and numeric values. An unchanged currently selected value creates no new observation. The aggregate does not replace latest or create an independent scan confirmation. Read all archive pages before completing comparison; tests cover the former 100,000-row market and 8,000-row item boundaries.

Archive storage verification: the live D1 service reported overload from full date scans. The schema adds the market/observation-time/key index through an additive migration. Collector and chart reads use an observation-time/key cursor in descending order, including ties, instead of repeatedly scanning earlier offsets. SQLite tests check use of the index without a temporary full sort and preserve more than 4,000 versions sharing one timestamp across the page boundary. Public responses retain a partial status and diagnostic logs on an actual read failure.
